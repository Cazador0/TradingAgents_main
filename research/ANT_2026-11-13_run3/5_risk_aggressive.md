Where I'd actually push back on the trader: the two-tranche structure ($250 at 1.05x, $250 at 1.15x) is the right shape, but using the full $500 day one instead of holding any back is the correct call given corroborated demand — sitting on dry powder "just in case" when the book is covered 6x is leaving edge on the table within rules that already cap the downside at ~$75 if the stop works. The neutral will call this a false binary; it isn't — the rules already force discipline (1.30x gate, 0.90x alert, 15% stop), so inside that cage, full allocation beats partial allocation. The real risk isn't sizing, it's hesitation: waiting past day one for a "named positive trigger" risks missing the only point where a stop-loss-defined $75 worst case is actually available, since week-two entries come without that same defined floor until a new average is set. I'd rather be wrong at a known, capped cost than cautious at an unknown opportunity cost.
