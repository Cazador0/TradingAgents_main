## Rebuttal to the bull case

**"6x oversubscribed" is not independent confirmation of healthy demand — it's one narrative repeated through multiple secondary channels.** The news analyst never traced this to the primary S-1/A or an underwriter statement; it's wire-to-wire reporting of "people familiar with the deal," and the sentiment analyst's StockTwits sample is just retail reposting the *same* figure back. Two channels repeating one unverified number isn't two independent confirmations — it's an echo. Heavy oversubscription at pricing is also a classic setup for a pop on day one followed by fade once the lock-up and valuation reality sets in, which argues for the user's existing stay-disciplined sizing, not a reason to add confidence.

**The $26B Class N compute-partner commitment cuts the other way.** The bull calls this a "demand floor," but it's related-party, non-market, non-lock-up-constrained capital — it inflates the headline raise and valuation optics without reflecting independent investor conviction. Worse, the sentiment report separately surfaces a "circular financing... beginning to collapse" bear thread tying Anthropic's compute-vendor deals to the broader AI-financing concern. That's a live, if low-frequency, risk the bull case doesn't address at all.

**Governance stability is a non-factor for a day-one pop/fade decision.** Founder control protects against multi-year strategy risk, not the only thing that matters here: first-week price action. It's irrelevant to whether 1.30x triggers or the stop gets hit.

**Macro backdrop is actively hostile, not neutral.** A hot inflation read already pushed the 10-year yield up and Nvidia down in the prior week; Polymarket prices a Fed December cut at just 1%. A richly valued, unprofitable-growth AI name is exactly what de-rates first in a higher-for-longer, risk-off tape — this is confirmed signal, not a gap.

**Unresolved lock-up carve-outs are a real overhang the bull ignores.** Up to 10% of non-founder locked shares reportedly can sell day one, with ~29% eligible near 180 days — unconfirmed against primary text, but if true it's incremental sell pressure into any early strength, right when a stop-loss would be most exposed to a gap-through.

**Data gaps compound the uncertainty**, not just "are there," per the user's own rules: no FRED data, conflicting retail-sales date, unconfirmed pricing/first-trade date (Nov 18/19 vs. "Thursday or Friday"). Flag, don't assume Nov 19 is locked.

**Net:** nothing here argues for exceeding the user's fixed caps — if anything, the macro and overhang risks argue for leaning toward the lower end of the day-one buys and treating the 0.90x alert and stop as load-bearing, not backstops.
