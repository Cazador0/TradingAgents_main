## Report
**The range cut cuts against the bull's own logic.** The bull calls the $70-$78 reset "pricing to clear," but the news report is explicit about *why* it happened: real investor pushback after the early-November tech selloff, with ambition falling from ~$1.5-2T to ~$1.3-1.4T. That's a demand problem being managed down to a level underwriters think they can still sell — not evidence of coiled upside. And the Polymarket shift the bull leans on cuts the other way too: probability mass moved *out of* the $1.75T+ brackets and into $1.25-1.75T, i.e., the market itself downgraded the odds of a big pop in the same week the range was cut. That's consistent with a flat-to-modest open, which is the worst case for this user: if ANT opens even moderately above 1.3x P, the rule says stay in cash, full stop — there's no "buy a little anyway" option.

**"Book covered" and "orders exceed shares" are not oversubscription multiples.** The news analyst flags this directly: no multiple was found anywhere, and both claims are secondary-source pickups. "Covered" at a range that was already cut once is a low bar — it describes a book that clears, not one straining for allocation. The bull's framing treats a minimum-demand signal as a maximum-demand one.

**The lock-up structure is a bear case dressed as a bull case.** Yes, only ~10% of non-founder locked shares are eligible day one — but the news report also notes demand registration rights attach to holders of up to 8.5B shares, and Class N (supplier-linked, Nvidia-adjacent-compute) shares sit entirely outside lock-up and outside public float. A thin, constrained float doesn't just support price on the way up — it means a buyer at open is pricing into a stock with a large reserve of future registered-resale supply overhanging it, with no trading history to show how it absorbs that supply.

**Revenue growth doesn't protect a price-insensitive buyer.** 14x YoY growth is a real number, but it's irrelevant to whether the *opening trade* clears above 1.3x P — a growth story justifies a valuation, it doesn't set the first print, which is a function of order-book mechanics and day-one sentiment, both of which the bull's own sources describe as softening (StockTwits bullish share down from 85% to 77% of labeled posts, more explicit profitability and circular-financing skepticism appearing).

**The bull dismisses the Nvidia/macro overhang too quickly.** "Unconfirmed resignation" doesn't make the turmoil irrelevant — the investigation is real, ongoing, and sits squarely on the compute-supplier/circular-financing linkage that the sentiment report's retail chatter is already connecting to ANT. Layer on ~85% odds of a December hike and hot CPI, and the macro backdrop is risk-off into the exact week ANT prices — a real tailwind for exactly the outcome this user's rule is designed to protect against: a hot, sentiment-driven open above 1.3x P that a disciplined buyer should refuse to chase, followed by potential downside the buyer can't get out of because the entry point was already disciplined into cash.
