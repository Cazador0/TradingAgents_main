## 2. Company Fundamentals

**What they do.** Standard Nuclear manufactures TRISO (tristructural-isotropic) coated-particle nuclear fuel and HALEU-based fuel forms — the fuel required by most next-generation small modular reactors (SMRs) and microreactors. It positions itself as the only U.S. company with industrial-scale, commercially available TRISO production.

**History.** Founded 2025 out of the bankruptcy of Ultra Safe Nuclear Corporation (USNC): Standard Nuclear acquired USNC's fuel assets for ~$28M, giving it a de-risked, already-operating production line at the Heritage Center (former K-25 site) in Oak Ridge for a fraction of replacement cost. Emerged from stealth June 2025 with a $42.5M seed (led by Decisive Point; a16z, Washington Harbour Partners and others participating) at a ~$150M valuation; raised a $140M Series A in January 2026 at an ~$838M valuation.

**Capacity.** ~19,000 sq ft facility producing ~500 kg TRISO/year, with expansion toward 2+ tonnes/year targeted by mid-2026. IPO proceeds fund further capacity build-out.

**Commercial traction.**
- First recipient of the U.S. DOE **Fuel Line Pilot Program**; Other Transaction Agreement for fuel production line authorization signed 2025-09-26 (a route to fuel qualification under DOE rather than a full NRC licensing path).
- **Framatome JV** ("Standard Nuclear × Framatome LLC", 66.7%/33.3%) to bring commercial-quantity TRISO products to market — meaningful validation from a tier-1 global fuel incumbent.
- Customers/partners: Radiant Industries, Antares, NANO Nuclear, Jimmy Energy, DOE national labs, Department of Defense.

**Financials (the hard part).**
- Trailing 12 months ended 2026-03-31: revenue **$3.36M**, net loss **$14.97M**.
- Q1 CY2026: revenue **$0.59M**, net loss **$7.7M** (loss rate accelerating as it scales).
- At the ~$3.3–3.55B target valuation this prices at roughly **~1,000x trailing sales**, and about **4x the Series A valuation set less than six months ago**.

## 3. News / Sector Analyst View

The macro backdrop is the strongest part of the story. AI data-center load growth (Goldman projects ~165% growth in data-center power demand by 2030) has re-rated the whole nuclear complex: Oklo is up ~700%+ over three years with a 14 GW pipeline and a binding 1.2 GW Meta agreement; uranium holds near $86/lb; U.S. policy (executive orders targeting a quadrupling of nuclear capacity, DOE HALEU allocation and pilot programs) is a direct tailwind for a domestic advanced-fuel supplier.

Recent nuclear IPO precedents cut both ways:
- **X-energy (XE, April 2026):** upsized $1.02B IPO at $23, +27% first-day pop — the closest comp (its TRISO-X subsidiary is Standard Nuclear's most direct competitor). But it later sold off hard on wider-than-expected losses, showing the market punishes pre-commercial names post-quiet-period.
- **Deep Fission (FISN, June 2026):** cut its range from $24–26 to price at $16, the low end of a *reduced* range — evidence the IPO window is selective, not indiscriminate.

## 4. Sentiment View

Retail and institutional enthusiasm for "picks-and-shovels" nuclear exposure is high, and STDN's narrative — *every* SMR/microreactor developer needs TRISO/HALEU fuel regardless of which reactor design wins — is an easy sell. The ~11% float plus eight-bank syndicate is engineered for a strong debut. Expect heavy first-day volatility and momentum-driven trading; the shares will trade on story, policy headlines, and contract announcements, not financials, for at least the first year.

## 5. Bull vs. Bear Debate

**Bull case**
1. Scarcity value: the only pure-play, exchange-listed U.S. TRISO/HALEU fuel manufacturer; fuel is the chokepoint of the entire advanced-reactor buildout.
2. Reactor-agnostic "arms dealer" economics — it wins whether Radiant, Antares, X-energy or DoD programs win, and demand is effectively policy-guaranteed via DOE pilot programs.
3. Real, operating production line acquired out of bankruptcy at ~$28M — years ahead of a greenfield entrant on fuel qualification and infrastructure.
4. Framatome JV and DOE Fuel Line Pilot selection are strong third-party validation.
5. Small float + red-hot sector + top-tier syndicate → favorable near-term supply/demand for the stock; X-energy's debut shows appetite.

**Bear case**
1. **Valuation:** ~$3.5B for $3.4M of trailing revenue (~1,000x sales) and widening losses; a 4x markup over its own January 2026 private round with little new fundamental information in between.
2. **Customer quality:** revenue depends on pre-revenue startups (Radiant, Antares, NANO, Jimmy) whose reactor timelines routinely slip; government demand is real but lumpy and appropriations-dependent.
3. **Competition:** X-energy's TRISO-X TF3 facility is purpose-built at larger scale for its own reactors; BWXT already produces TRISO for DoD programs; Kairos and others pursue in-house fuel. "Only industrial-scale producer" is a claim with a shelf life.
4. **Feedstock risk:** HALEU supply is a national bottleneck (Centrus is essentially the only domestic enricher); Standard Nuclear's throughput depends on material it does not control.
5. **Governance:** 20:1 dual-class with a founder holding ~59.5% of votes; minority Class A holders have no effective say.
6. **Precedent:** the predecessor company (USNC) went bankrupt in this exact business; X-energy's post-IPO selloff and Deep Fission's slashed pricing show how quickly sentiment mean-reverts once losses print.
7. Lock-up expiry (~January 2027) puts a large overhang against a small float.

## 6. Risk Manager Synthesis

This is a **story-stock IPO with a genuinely strategic asset underneath it, priced for flawless execution**. The asset (operating TRISO line + DOE anchor + Framatome JV) is real and scarce; the price already capitalizes several years of successful scale-up. Expected first-day/first-month trading is favorable (hot sector, tiny float, strong syndicate), but the 6–18 month fundamental risk/reward at ~$3.3B+ is poor unless contracted backlog grows an order of magnitude. Position sizing, not conviction, is the correct control here.

## 7. Decision

**Rating (five-tier): HOLD — speculative BUY only at/near the IPO price for risk-tolerant accounts, small size.**

- **If offered allocation at $18–21:** attractive as a short-horizon speculative position (sector momentum + 11% float + X-energy precedent favor a pop). Size it as venture-style risk capital — assume the position can lose 50%+.
- **Chasing the aftermarket:** avoid paying a large premium above range on day one; the Deep Fission and post-IPO X-energy tape shows these names round-trip. Better fundamental entry points typically appear after the quiet period ends and around the ~6-month lock-up expiry (~January 2027), or after the first earnings print as a public company.
- **For fundamentals-driven / long-only mandates:** pass at this valuation; revisit when contracted revenue/backlog, HALEU feedstock agreements, and Framatome JV volumes are disclosed in the first 10-Qs.

**Key monitorables:** final pricing vs. range (above-range pricing = demand signal), DOE Fuel Line Pilot milestones, Framatome JV purchase commitments, HALEU feedstock supply agreements, capacity ramp to 2 t/yr, cash burn vs. ~$400M post-IPO cash, and lock-up expiry.
